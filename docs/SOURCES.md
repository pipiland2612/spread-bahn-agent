## Rules that affect the first implementation

- Level 1 permits one car and one fee step per gig. Unbooked gigs can be repriced.
- At Levels 1–3 every quote wins the first option at its list price. Decline by sending no quote.
- Every order must have all its cars delivered on time to count as on time. At least one won order and 90% on time are required for a ranked week.
- Each ranking round has five secret weeks at each level. All five must meet the delivery minimum for a team rank. Total margin determines rank.
- Ranked attempts cannot be repeated or revealed. Deleting an unfinished attempt still consumes it and lets it finish without the agent.
- Practice worlds pause between advances. Up to eight unfinished worlds may coexist on the team key.
- The final is Level 8 and uses a live clock. Success at Level 1 is an integration milestone, not evidence of final readiness.
