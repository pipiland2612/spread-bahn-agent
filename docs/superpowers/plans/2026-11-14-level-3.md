## Global Constraints

- No dependencies or vendor changes. Levels 1/2 behavior remains unchanged.
- Three modes: baseline, offers, chains. Baseline remains default until held-out promotion.
- Offer limits: three per driver/day, three open per gig, no more than three lifetime attempts per gig, one queued offer per gig/tick, no offers 23:00–06:59.
- Chains: two cars maximum, at most 15 minutes deadhead, at most 360 road minutes, same-day feasible, 55-minute approach, 15-minute stop buffers, 210-minute initial slack, 40-minute charging where required.
- At most four unfinished worlds; preserve all attempts and credentials.
- Promotion requires ≥95% on time every week, positive total margin, greater held-out total than baseline, zero refused actions, complete evidence.

## Review Focus

- Multiple deliveries in one gig must keep it working until all remaining legs finish.
- Cancellation or expiry must preserve/repost surviving work without duplicate coverage.
- Restart during an offer's ten-minute lifetime must not reset daily limits or duplicate the offer.
- Queued offers must be removed when booking occurs later in the same event batch.
- A chain crossing closing time must be rejected even if summed road minutes are short.
